Three windows, one question each.

Built from the design system’s bundle.css and tokens, at size-win-m 720px like Findings. Findings is indexed on files and the Vault on secrets; these three are indexed on programs and the files they read, and each earns its place by saying something no other window says. Every number is this Mac’s, from jit status, jit audit and the last deep scan; nothing is a value.

the rule
  • one questionA window answers one question and owns the facts nobody else has. AI Agents: what has this agent seen, what can it reach, what did it do. Tools: what runs through jit, and is it working. Decoys: which files serve them, who read them, and which are still in the open.
  • the headerThe Findings shape: the good news, then one line per thing to do, in the cards’ own numbers. a number in the header is a number on a card.
  • before, afterA key in the open is a Finding wherever it is seen; a wrapped tool, a captured session, a served decoy have no other home. So the before-verbs are Findings’ and the after-verbs live here.
  • testWould the reader change what they do after reading it? Last use, who else read it, what an agent has seen, a missing vault entry: yes. Version strings, catalogue counts, percentages: no.

what changes
  • bug, nowAI Agents counts every finding in the agent’s files. Vault copies, cached copies and shape-found tokens, not one type. Ships on its own before any redesign; on this Mac the row goes red.
  • enginejit scan asks the keychain. wrap list --discover’s probe joins the scan so a keychain-held token is a Finding with jit wrap gh as its fix. Then Tools lists no unwrapped tools, and “why doesn’t scan find the tools to wrap” closes at its root.
  • engineServe events name the reader. The audit already tries; on this Mac every read this week says nothing. The row prints “reader not recorded” until it does.
  • engineConsent events per program. Asked, allowed, denied, with the program and the secret names; the card needs them by program.
  • engineA per-agent redact switch. The Settings switch is global; the card offers it per agent, as a switch, with its cost on the row’s second line.
  • D10AI Agents acts on its own rows. Clean Caches and Redact sit on the “In its files” row: the same commands, the same dialogs, as Findings runs. D10 said “a digest, not a place to act”; the digest was wrong because nobody looked at it, and a window nobody acts from is not looked at.
  • removedScan Now… and the Asking footer on AI Agents. Scanning is Findings’ verb; the header sentence says when the last scan ran and links there. “Asking is on” was a setting’s name in a footer whose only verb sent you to Settings; it is now a fact on the Can reach row (“a real value goes to it only after your Touch ID”) with its switch when off, and the footer states: “1 agent · 34 copies in its files · 44 runs this week”. Findings… on the card title duplicated the row’s verbs and moved into ···.
  • removedThe catalogue, Open in Terminal, Refresh as a button. Refresh is the ··· menu’s; the window reloads when it opens.
  • nameDecoys keeps its name. It is the one word in the panel that says what the protection does rather than what it is, and it is the thing a reader gets. “Mount” stays the CLI’s word. The window’s headline carries the file count, so the name does not have to.
  • windows.mdThe header’s to-do lines are the one region addition. Findings ships them (jit-app #44); this page uses them the same way. windows.md should gain the lines: app-row 13, a dot with its word, the verb an app-action link, space-app-2 apart.
