JitPass Setup

A full scan needs Full Disk Access

macOS keeps Desktop, Documents and Downloads closed to every app until you open them. Without this switch it would ask you folder by folder. JitPass only reads, and only on this Mac.

1Open System Settings. It lands on the right page.
2Turn on JitPass in the list.
3Come back. The scan starts by itself.
