what changes
  • window720 wide, opening at the height of its content. Today it is 640 with a 480 minimum, and it opens 520 tall with half of that empty. Scan is a size-win-m window in the system's table, and 720 is what makes a row's name, fact and action group fit on one line.
  • title bar"Scan", not "JitPass Scan". The app is already in the menu bar. The five buttons leave the headline row: Rescan sits in the header's action group, the rest go in ···.
  • headerThe count, once, with the limit under it. A 34px status mark in the state's colour, the headline at 15/600, and one sentence saying what jit read and what would change the answer. The ten-cell bar becomes the system's 6px progress bar, and only when the score is short of 100%.
  • sectionsThree headings become three cards with eyebrows. A dot and one word carry the tier, so a tier with nothing in it simply has no card, and "nothing" is never printed. The counts live in the filter and the footer.
  • rowsOne row per file: name, one fact, two verbs. Filename first, folder after it in mono, so four rows never open with the same six words. Open is the safe verb, Protect… is the one that asks, and Reveal, Copy Path and Ignore move into ··· where a mis-click costs nothing.
  • linesA file's flagged lines open in a sheet. Six lines under one row was a fourth level of nesting. The row says "6 flagged lines · AWS Access Key ID, SendGrid API Key, JWT and 3 more" and the sheet lists them.
  • filterA segmented filter, only when there is something to narrow. Zero-count tiers are omitted, never greyed. A clean whole-Mac scan has no toolbar at all.
  • footerWhat was found on the left, the one action on the right. Protect All is the window's default button and lives here.
  • removedOpen in Terminal is gone. The system's rule: a footer that re-runs the thing the window is named after says the window did not work. jit scan stays in the terminal for people who live there.
  • removedGrant Full Disk Access stops being a button. It is a standing limit, so it belongs in the header sentence, with a quiet Grant Access… beside Rescan while it is missing.
to confirm
  • not in windows.mdThe progress bar in the header. The header is specified as mark, headline, sentence and actions, nothing else. The coverage bar is the score's one picture, so I put it under the sentence at 320px. If you'd rather keep the header strict, it moves into a card, or goes away and the headline carries it alone.
  • not in windows.mdTwo buttons in the empty state. The spec allows one quiet button; scanning genuinely has two answers (a folder, or the whole Mac), so the default takes the whole Mac and the quiet one opens the picker.
